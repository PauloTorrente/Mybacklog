<p align="center">
  <samp>
    An interactive showcase of my physical game collection —<br />
    Switch, 3DS, DS, Wii and PS4 — built as a love letter to the Wii and DS era.<br />
    3D cases. Glass shelves. Everything you'd expect from a Nintendo kid.
  </samp>

---

- **Pick a console** — every console is a 3D cube you rotate and click, sliding in from the wardrobe rail.
- **Browse the shelf** — each game stands as a physical case with front, spine, back and plastic edges, all in 3D.
- **Flip the case** — click a game to see its back cover, because a shelf isn't a shelf if you can't peek at the back.
- **See what's missing** — wishlist games stand greyed out and faded, so the gap is always visible.
- **Filter by genre** — pills, a distribution bar, and a URL that remembers your filters. Share a link, open the same shelf.
- **amiibo shelves** — 51 figures across 8 series, scaled so every base matches and couples stand together.
- **Three languages** — Portuguese, Spanish and English, switchable from the top bar.
